GET TO KNOW US

EDUCATION THAT GOES BEYOND.

Marwadi University is a private university located in Rajkot, Gujarat. Established as a university in 2016, it provides education across engineering, management, science, law, pharmacy, computer applications and several other disciplines.

The university focuses on academic learning, innovation, research, entrepreneurship and industry-oriented education. Its campus offers academic facilities and opportunities for students to participate in cultural, technical and sports activities.
